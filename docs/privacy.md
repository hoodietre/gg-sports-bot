# GG Sports — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information GG Sports ("we," "us,"
"our") collects when you use the GG Sports Discord bot and related
services (the "Service"), and how that information is used, stored, and
protected.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**1.1 Discord-provided information.** When GG Sports is added to a Server
or used by a member of a Server, we receive and may store:
- Discord user IDs (yours and other members' where relevant to league
  data — e.g., team ownership, roster assignments, trade history)
- Discord Server (guild) IDs
- Discord usernames/display names, where needed to display league
  information (e.g., leaderboards, rosters, transaction logs)
- Message content only where directly submitted to a GG Sports command
  (e.g., text entered into a command option); we do not read or store
  general Server chat.

**1.2 League and game data.** GG Sports stores league configuration and
data you or your league members provide or that is synced from supported
third-party game platforms (e.g., Madden export data), including but not
limited to: rosters, transactions, standings, stats, draft results, trade
history, and related league records.

**1.3 Billing information.** If a Server subscribes to Premium, billing is
handled by Stripe, Inc. We do not directly collect or store full payment
card numbers. We do store:
- The Discord Server ID and Discord user ID of the subscribing owner/
  billing manager, linked to a Stripe customer/subscription identifier
- Subscription status, tier, billing period, and renewal dates
- Billing-related events needed to operate the subscription (e.g.,
  payment success/failure status, cancellation date)

Stripe's handling of your payment details is governed by Stripe's own
privacy policy, available at stripe.com/privacy.

**1.4 Automatically collected information.** We may log technical
information related to bot operation, such as command usage, error logs,
and timestamps, for the purpose of debugging and improving the Service.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:
- Operate and provide GG Sports' features, including league tracking,
  statistics, and Premium functionality
- Process and manage subscriptions, trials, and billing, including trial
  eligibility tracking by Discord user ID (to prevent duplicate free
  trial claims across Servers)
- Communicate with Server owners and billing managers about their
  subscription (e.g., trial ending, payment failure, cancellation
  confirmation)
- Diagnose technical issues and improve reliability and features
- Enforce these Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy

We do not sell your information to third parties. We do not use your data
to serve advertising.

## 3. How We Share Information

We share information only in the following circumstances:
- **With Stripe**, to process payments and manage subscriptions
- **With third-party data sources you connect** (e.g., a Madden/EA
  account link), solely to retrieve the league data you've requested
- **With Discord**, as an inherent part of operating a Discord bot (all
  bot activity necessarily passes through Discord's platform, which has
  its own data practices)
- **If required by law**, such as in response to a valid legal request
- **In connection with a business transfer**, such as a merger,
  acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case affected users would be
  notified

We do not share your data with other third parties for their own
marketing purposes.

## 4. Data Retention

- **Active Servers**: league data is retained for as long as GG Sports
  remains active in the Server.
- **Lapsed Premium subscriptions**: where Premium-tier data exceeds Free
  Tier limits, that data is locked (not deleted) for a grace period of
  30 days following a full subscription lapse, as
  described in our Terms of Service. If Premium is not reactivated within
  that period, the excess data may be permanently deleted.
- **Server removal**: if GG Sports is removed from a Server, associated
  league data may be retained for up to 30 days in case the bot is
  re-added, after which it may be deleted.
- **Billing records**: retained as required for tax, accounting, and
  legal compliance purposes, independent of Service usage.

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or
request deletion of your personal information. To make such a request,
contact us at hoodietre@gmail.com. Note that some information (such as
league records involving other members) may be retained or anonymized
rather than fully deleted where it is shared data belonging to a Server
rather than solely to you.

Server owners can remove GG Sports from their Server at any time via
Discord's standard app management tools, and may request full deletion of
their Server's data by contacting us.

## 6. Children's Privacy

GG Sports is not directed at children and is intended for use in
accordance with Discord's own minimum age requirements. We do not
knowingly collect information from users who do not meet Discord's
eligibility requirements.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the
information we store. No method of transmission or storage is completely
secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. International Data Transfers

GG Sports' infrastructure, including our database, is hosted through Railway, a cloud hosting provider. Our servers are located in the European Union (EU West — Amsterdam, Netherlands). Depending on your country of residence, this means your information is processed and stored outside of your home country, within the European Union. By using GG Sports, you consent to this transfer, processing, and storage of your information outside of your country of residence.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will
be communicated where reasonably practicable. Continued use of GG Sports
after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## 10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to hoodietre@gmail.com.
