trial conversion, or failed payment as described in Section 4.5) and the

- Data and configurations exceeding Free Tier limits become locked

Locked data is retained for a grace period of 30
days following a full lapse. If Premium is not reactivated within that
grace period, locked data exceeding Free Tier limits may be permanently
deleted. We will make reasonable efforts to notify the Server owner and any
billing manager before permanent deletion occurs.
